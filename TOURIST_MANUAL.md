Welcome to **Boost Baguio**! This guide is designed to help you, as a tourist, navigate the City of Pines with ease. Whether you are figuring out which jeepney to ride, looking for the best places to eat, or checking out local events, Boost Baguio is your digital companion.

This manual will walk you through every feature of the app so you can make the most out of your trip.

## 1. Getting Started (Account Setup)

While you can use most of Boost Baguio without an account, creating a free account allows you to **save your favorite places** and access premium features like **saved routes**.

**How to Sign Up:**

1. Click on **Login** at the top right corner of the screen.
2. Select **Sign Up**.
3. Enter your details (Email, Name, and Password).
4. Click **Create Account**.

Once logged in, you can click on your profile icon in the top right to visit your `/account` page to edit your profile or view your saved items.

## 2. APANAM — Plan Your Jeepney Trip

_Navigate to:_ `/apanam`

Baguio is famous for its colorful jeepneys, but knowing which one to ride can be confusing. **APANAM** (Ilocano for "Where are you going?") is your personal jeepney routing tool.

**How to get directions:**

1. **Set your Starting Point (FROM):**
   - The app will automatically try to detect your current location. Your browser will ask for location permissions—please click **Allow**.
   - If the GPS button next to the input is **Green**, your location is locked in. If it is **Gray**, you can click it to try detecting your location again.
   - _Prefer to type it manually?_ Click the "FROM" text box, type your location (e.g., "SM City Baguio"), and press Enter.
2. **Set your Destination (TO):**
   - Click the "TO" dropdown and search or select your destination.
3. **Start Navigation:**
   - Click the **Start Navigation** button.
   - The app will suggest different jeepney routes (including options that require a single ride or a transfer).
4. **Follow the Route:**
   - Pick an option to view it on the map. The map uses color-coded markers to guide you:
     - 🟢 **Green:** Your starting point.
     - 🔵 **Blue:** Where to board your first jeepney (Terminal/Stop).
     - 🟡 **Yellow:** Where to get off and transfer (if a second jeepney is needed).
     - 🟣 **Purple:** Where to board your second jeepney.
     - 🔴 **Red:** Your final drop-off point.
5. **Read the Step-by-Step Guide:** Scroll below the map for easy-to-read, numbered instructions on how to reach your destination.

## 3. PAGNAAM — Browse All Jeepney Routes

_Navigate to:_ `/pagnaam`

If you just want to familiarize yourself with the local transport lines, use **PAGNAAM** (Ilocano for "Where you walk/travel").

**How to use it:**

- **Search for a Jeepney:** Use the search bar to find a specific route (e.g., "Mines View", "Trancoville").
- **View Jeepney Details:** Click on any jeepney card to open its details. Here you will find:
  - **Fares & Operating Hours:** Know how much to pay and until what time the jeepney runs.
  - **Identification Tips:** Helpful hints on how to recognize the jeepney (e.g., specific colors or signages).
  - **Route Map & Stops:** A full map of the route (the polyline) along with a numbered list of all the major stops along the way.

## 4. MAYKAN — Discover Places to Visit

_Navigate to:_ `/maykan`

**MAYKAN** (Ilocano for "Come here") is your directory for Baguio's best tourist spots, restaurants, and accommodations.

**How to use it:**

- **Filter by Category:** Use the filter chips at the top to narrow down your search (e.g., only show "Tourist Spots" or "Restaurants").
- **View Place Details:** Click on a place's card to see its photo, exact address, operating hours, and contact information.
- **Get Directions:** Found a place you like? Click **Get Directions** inside the popup. This will send you straight to the APANAM tool with your destination already filled in!
- **Save for Later:** If you are logged in, click **Save Place** to bookmark it. You can view all your bookmarked spots later in your account dashboard.

## 5. ARAMIDEM — Check Local Events

_Navigate to:_ `/aramidem`

Don't miss out on what's happening during your stay! **ARAMIDEM** (Ilocano for "What you do") is a calendar of local events, festivals, and pop-ups.

**How to use it:**

- **Browse the Calendar:** View events by month or switch to a list view.
- **Event Details:** Click on any event to see what it's about, dates, and times.
- **Get Directions:** Just like with places, you can click **Get Directions** to instantly find out how to commute to the event venue.

## 6. AYAN MO — Find What's Nearby

_Navigate to:_ `/ayanmo`

If you are already out and about and want to know what's around you, use **AYAN MO** (Ilocano for "Where are you?").

**How to use it:**

- **Allow Location Access:** The app will detect where you are and center the map on you. (You can also manually type a location in the search bar).
- **Filter What You Want to See:** Use the floating menu on the right to select multiple categories (e.g., select "Restaurants" and "Cafes").
- **Adjust the Search Radius:** Want to walk? Set the radius to **500m** or **1km**. Willing to commute? Expand it to **2km** or **5km**.
- **Interactive Map:** Hover over any marker on the map to see the place's name, or click on it for full details.
- **Live List:** The list on the side updates in real-time based on the map and your filters.

## 7. Managing Your Profile & Saved Items

As a logged-in user, you have access to a personal dashboard.

- **Edit Profile:** Go to `/account` to update your name or change your password.
- **View Saved Places:** In the `/account` section, you will find all the places you bookmarked in MAYKAN.
- **Saved Routes (Premium Feature):** If you have access to premium features, navigate to `/saved-routes` to find your frequently used jeepney routes from APANAM so you don't have to search for them again.

## 8. Troubleshooting & Tips

- **My location isn't updating!**
  Ensure your browser/phone settings allow location access for the website. If it still fails, click the **GPS button** next to the location input bar, or simply type your location manually (e.g., "Burnham Park").
- **The map is blank!**
  Make sure you have an active internet connection. If you are on a public Wi-Fi (like in a mall or hotel), you might need to sign into their portal first.
- **I can't find a jeepney route for my destination.**
  Some obscure locations may not be fully mapped yet. Try setting your destination to the nearest major landmark (e.g., a nearby school, park, or major intersection).
- **How much should I pay the driver?**
  Base fares for jeepneys in the Philippines change occasionally. Always check the **PAGNAAM** tab for the latest estimated fare of the jeepney you are riding.
